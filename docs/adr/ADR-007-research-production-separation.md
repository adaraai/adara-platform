# ADR-007: Research is not a dependency

## Status

Accepted (foundation)

## Context

ADARA must grow from a small team to an AI lab without rewriting the org every quarter.

## Decision

Notebooks and papers live in adara-research. Production must vendor a reviewed artifact, not import notebooks.

## Consequences

Documented in ADARA-ARCHITECTURE.md. Revisit when a team actually hits the limitation.
