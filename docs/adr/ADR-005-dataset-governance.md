# ADR-005: Consent-first datasets

## Status

Accepted (foundation)

## Context

ADARA must grow from a small team to an AI lab without rewriting the org every quarter.

## Decision

Every dataset needs a card. No scraping private chats. Compensation and takedown are first-class.

## Consequences

Documented in ADARA-ARCHITECTURE.md. Revisit when a team actually hits the limitation.
