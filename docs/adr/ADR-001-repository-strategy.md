# ADR-001: Hybrid repository strategy

## Status

Accepted (foundation)

## Context

ADARA must grow from a small team to an AI lab without rewriting the org every quarter.

## Decision

Tightly coupled apps/services live in adara-platform. Independent release/ownership domains get their own repo.

## Consequences

Documented in ADARA-ARCHITECTURE.md. Revisit when a team actually hits the limitation.
